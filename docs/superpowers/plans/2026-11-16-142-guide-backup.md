# Guide: Backup and Upgrades Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use
> superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or

**Goal:** Plan 142 of [the step 6 index](2026-11-16-00-roadmap-step-6.md): the
guide tells the operator how much disk to give the instance, what to back up and
how, how to restore, how to upgrade, and how to go back after an upgrade.

**Architecture:** Documents only. Three sections of `docs/wiki/deployment.md`:

- **Sizing** (NF-1.7): working copies per user, so ten users of one 2 GB remote
  use 20 GB; the formula _sum over users of their repositories' sizes, plus the
  low-space threshold, plus about 10% for `tmp/` during clones, plus the
  database and `backups/`_ _(plan's call: the exact form)_, with one worked
  example.
- **Backup and restore** (NF-6.1 to NF-6.6): the volumes and the secrets outside
  them; the daily snapshot; a running copy vs a stopped one; what a working-copy
  backup protects; restoring, optionally from a snapshot; `orphans/` after a
  restore; the slow first start after a restore (already in Troubleshooting).
- **Upgrading** (NF-7.1 to NF-7.7): pull the new tag, read the release notes,
  restart; the pre-upgrade snapshot; no downgrade; going back by restoring that
  snapshot with the old image; downtime during the restart.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown.

**Spec:** `docs/architecture/non-functional.md` NF-1.7, NF-6, NF-7;
`docs/architecture/data.md` A-4.12 (file names, as plan 129 built them).
